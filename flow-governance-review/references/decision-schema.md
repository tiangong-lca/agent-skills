# Flow decision notes

Record the exact candidate identities/versions, observed scientific differences, evidence, decision (reuse, keep distinct, or unresolved), rationale and authorized scope. These are caller-owned notes, not an executable CLI materialization schema. A canonical choice alone does not authorize deleting other Flows or rewriting consumers.

Inspect selected Flow content and outgoing references using typed get/list-references. Prepare any agreed candidate as canonical content or an explicit patch, then inspect diff and validate. Separate create/save/delete requests require current actor/environment and target preconditions. Missing reverse-consumer evidence must remain visible; there is no automatic alias propagation or regeneration operation.
