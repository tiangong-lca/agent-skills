# Evidence extraction and gap assessment

Use this guidance to turn materials into candidate inventory facts, independently of database writes. Input is the agreed goal/boundary plus source files and their inventory; output is a readable evidence table and prioritized questions for foreground modeling.

For each quantity preserve the source location, original value and unit, operation and exchange direction, product/reference denominator, period/geography and evidence class. Show a derivation when converting a monthly total to a unit product. Do not treat a name match as a Flow identity, a blank as zero, a purchase as a measured emission, or an uncertainty estimate as a measured value.

Check coverage of the stated boundary, double counting across totals and submetering, product versus waste meaning, physical units, production output/losses and allocation bases. Keep contradictions attached to both sources. Ask which source or period applies when the answer changes the result; use a labelled scenario only with an explicit reason. Do not fabricate a number to complete a model.

Prioritize questions by their effect on the Model: missing functional quantity or major energy/material amount may prevent calculation; optional narrative context need not stop independent modeling. A proposed proxy remains a proposal with selection reasoning until adopted. Return traceable candidate Process exchanges, unresolved data questions and explicit assumptions, without imposing content hashes or freezing the whole research object.
